Creator Portfolio

I joined after the vision sprint had established the opportunity to bring Creator Portfolio (a.k.a. media kits) to Instagram. I owned the product strategy for how this should work natively on the platform—from defining the creator experience and design principles to aligning stakeholders and driving execution.

Key Results

0× Adoption Goal
0% Creator Activation
0K Creators Onboarded
Challenge

Small creators couldn't get heard — and brands couldn't find them.

To get noticed, creators — especially first-time earners — had to chase follower counts, then pitch brands with scattered off-platform media kits. Brands had the mirror problem: they wanted smaller, niche creators but couldn't find or evaluate them.

Media kits were already an industry standard.

By bringing them natively to Instagram, less experienced creators could present themselves professionally without leaving the platform, giving brands more confidence to discover and evaluate them.

Strategy

Make participation effortless

Rather than asking creators to invest significant time building a professional media kit, I focused on making portfolio creation almost effortless. Lowering the upfront investment encouraged more creators to participate, giving brands more creators to discover and creating the foundation for a healthy two-sided marketplace.

Creator action Brand action

The flywheel I mapped at the outset — how a single portfolio feature could compound into a healthier two-sided marketplace.

Approach

Effortless to create, easy to be found.

Auto-populate creators' best content so they never start from a blank template, then make publishing one tap so every portfolio is immediately discoverable in Creator Marketplace.

01

Never start from empty

Auto-populate each portfolio with the creator's top content and insights — edit and polish, never start from a blank page.

02

Creator-led story

Beyond the auto-populated basics, creators could add their own content and voice — so each portfolio told an authentic, creator-led story.

03

Create once, discover everywhere

Publish once, and the portfolio automatically appears in Creator Marketplace where brands discover creators.

Craft

Reuse familiar patterns; invent only where it pays off.

For less-experienced creators, every extra decision is friction. So I pre-filled the parts that intimidate people and built the rest on patterns creators already knew — reserving a brand-new pattern for the one place the familiar one fell flat.

The template fills itself in

Each portfolio opens pre-populated with the creator’s best content and audience data — top posts and Highlights, selected reels and stories, reach and engagement insights, past brand partnerships, even audience demographics — pulled straight from their account. Instead of a blank page, a less-experienced creator starts from a credible draft they only need to edit.

Built on Guides, not a new builder

I reused Instagram’s existing Guides pattern — curate a post, add a short description, arrange the sequence — so the portfolio builder felt native from day one and shipped without asking creators to learn anything new.

A new onboarding format, earned

The intro was the exception. A bullet-point explainer felt flat, so I replaced it with a short video of a real creator walking through how they built their portfolio. It tested more engaging — and the Subscriptions team later adopted the same format for their own launches.

MVP experience

An on-platform media kit, built into Instagram.

A single, shareable profile where creators curate their best posts, performance insights, and past collaborations — so their work, not their follower count, makes the pitch.

Reflections

Discovery was the real work.

Adoption was never the bottleneck

Creators of every size embraced it instantly — 13× the goal in two hours. The real gap was brand-side: they weren't discovering portfolios in their workflows.

ML closed the brand-side gap

The team then invested in ML-powered discovery to surface creators where brands work — driving 20–72% more portfolio and profile views.

The portfolio endures

Creator Portfolio is still core to the marketplace — now growing 600% year over year.
