Creator Portfolio

Creators had no on-platform way to show brands their value. I designed Creator Portfolio — an on-platform media kit where creators curate their best posts, insights, and past collaborations to pitch themselves to brands.

Key Results

0× Adoption Goal
0% Creator Activation
0K Creators Onboarded
Challenge

Creators couldn't prove their value — and brands couldn't evaluate it.

Instagram's creator marketplace connected brands with creators, but the two sides couldn't speak the same language. Creators pitched themselves through scattered screenshots and off-platform media kits, cobbling their value together across DMs, decks, and third-party tools — nothing lived where the work actually did.

On the other side, brands had no consistent, trustworthy view of a creator's best content, audience, and past partnerships — making it hard to judge fit at a glance.

Hypothesis

A native, curated profile would help creators pitch — and brands evaluate.

If creators could present their value natively on Instagram — one curated profile of their best work, insights, and past collaborations — they'd pitch themselves more effectively, and brands would get a consistent, trustworthy way to evaluate them. The bet was to meet both sides where the work already lived, rather than sending them off-platform.

Approach

Two levers for adoption: onboarding and discovery.

For the portfolio to take off, two things had to be true — creators had to actually build one, and they had to find the feature in the first place. I focused the approach on onboarding and discoverability.

Onboard with a creator, not bullet points

Instead of Instagram's standard onboarding screen — a list of bullet points — I proposed a NUX video featuring a creator explaining the benefits of Creator Portfolio, showing the value rather than listing it.

Multiple entry points

I explored multiple entry points into the feature to maximize discoverability, so creators could find and start their portfolio wherever they already were on Instagram.

The onboarding NUX — a creator explaining the value of Creator Portfolio, in place of a bullet-point screen.

Craft

Designing past the two biggest drop-off points.

The riskiest moments were the empty template and the fear of exposing unfinished work. Two design decisions addressed them.

Never start from empty

I proposed auto-populating each portfolio with the creator's popular content and key insights, so they could edit and polish quickly instead of staring at a blank template.

Private by default

A visibility banner — “Your portfolio is only visible to you” — reassured creators their work was private, empowering them to keep polishing until they were ready to share it with brands.

Solution

An on-platform media kit, built into Instagram.

I designed Creator Portfolio — a space where creators curate their best posts, performance insights, and past brand collaborations into a single, shareable profile. It turned scattered self-promotion into one credible, native pitch a creator could send to any brand.

Creators curate posts, insights, and collaborations into a shareable, on-platform media kit.

Visuals recreated to respect Meta confidentiality.

Impact

Massive creator adoption — 13× the launch goal.

Creator Portfolio launched in November 2022 and hit its adoption goal within the first two hours — then kept climbing, driving a step-change in how creators activated on the marketplace.

13× adoption goal

59.6K portfolios published by the end of H2 ’22 — 13× the 5,000 goal, which we hit within the first two hours of launch.

60% creator activation

Creator activation reached 60%, up from a ~25% prior benchmark, with 126K creators activated.

216K creators onboarded

10× the previous half — a step-change in creator adoption of the marketplace.

Portfolios were shared 22K+ times and drew 53K total views since launch.

The launch campaign — “It Pays to Be You.”

Reflections

What I Learned

Adoption was never the bottleneck

Creators embraced the portfolio instantly — 13× the goal, hit within two hours. The real gap was on the brand side: brands weren't discovering these portfolios inside their workflows.

Discovery completes the ecosystem

The team invested in ML-powered discovery to surface creator content where brands work — driving 20–72% more portfolio and profile views and closing the loop.

The portfolio endures

Creator Portfolio remains a core part of the creator marketplace ecosystem — a product now growing 600% year over year.
